Our Story

About Us

DFW Rentals & Event Planning is a boutique event company built on one simple promise: every client gets a dedicated specialist who sees their event through from first call to final takedown. In our first year of operation, we've delivered rentals and planning support for weddings, corporate gatherings, and private celebrations — with a track record of on-time setups, zero no-shows, and clients who come back for their next event.

Though we're based in the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex, our reach extends internationally. Whether you're planning locally or coordinating a destination event abroad, we bring the same hands-on service, attention to detail, and reliable delivery that our clients rely on.

What sets us apart isn't just what we rent — it's how we work. Clear communication, honest timelines, and a single point of contact who knows your event inside and out.
